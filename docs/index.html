Privacy Policy

Detergent Inventory  ·  Last updated: September 2026

This privacy policy describes how the Detergent Inventory mobile application ("the App") handles your information. The App is designed to be simple, private, and to keep your data entirely under your control.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App does not require you to create an account, provide an email address, or submit any personal details to us.

Data You Enter

The App allows you to record inventory and sales information, including:

  • Client names you enter
  • Dates of sales
  • Quantities of items sold
  • Payment methods you select
  • Types of cleaning supplies you categorize

All of this information is stored locally on your device only, using Apple's standard on-device storage. It is never transmitted to us, to any server, or to any third party. It never leaves your device.

Analytics, Tracking, and Advertising

The App contains no analytics tools, no tracking technologies, and no advertising. We do not track your usage, your location, or your behavior.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party services, SDKs, or software development kits that collect data.

Children's Privacy

The App does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13. It is safe for users of all ages.

Data Deletion

Because all data is stored only on your device, you can delete all App data at any time by deleting the App from your device. No copies remain anywhere else.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, an updated version will be posted at this same URL. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the change.

Contact
If you have any questions about this policy, you can reach out at: your-email@example.com
