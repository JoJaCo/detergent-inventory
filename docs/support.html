Detergent Inventory is a simple tool for tracking bucket inventory and sales.

Common Questions

How do I add buckets to my inventory?
Tap "Add Buckets" on the main screen, enter the amount, and select the supply type.

Where is my data stored?
All data is stored locally on your device. Nothing is sent to any server.

How do I delete my data?
Deleting the app from your device removes all stored data.
